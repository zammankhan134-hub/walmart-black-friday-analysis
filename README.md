# Walmart Black Friday Sales Analysis
### Customer Purchase Behavior | Central Limit Theorem (CLT) | Confidence Intervals

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior during Walmart's Black Friday sales using Python and statistical techniques.

The primary objective is to investigate whether female customers spend more than male customers and understand how customer demographics influence purchasing behavior.

The analysis focuses on statistical distributions, sampling, confidence intervals, and customer segmentation to generate actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
- Compare average purchase amounts between male and female customers.
- Understand the Central Limit Theorem using sampling techniques.
- Calculate confidence intervals for customer groups.
- Analyze purchasing behavior across age groups.
- Examine spending patterns based on marital status.
- Generate data-driven business recommendations.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains Walmart customer transaction information.

Key features include:

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Gender | Customer gender |
| Age | Customer age group |
| Occupation | Customer occupation |
| City_Category | Customer city category |
| Marital_Status | Customer marital status |
| Purchase | Purchase amount |

Each row represents a customer purchase.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning
- Examined dataset quality.
- Checked missing values.
- Prepared data for statistical analysis.

### 2. Purchase Distribution Analysis
- Analyzed purchase amount distribution.
- Identified right-skewness.
- Examined high-value purchases and outliers.

### 3. Gender-Based Analysis
- Compared male and female purchasing behavior.
- Examined differences in average purchase amounts.
- Visualized spending distributions.

### 4. Central Limit Theorem (CLT)
- Generated multiple samples from the dataset.
- Calculated sample means.
- Examined the distribution of sample means.
- Observed an approximately normal distribution.

### 5. Confidence Interval Analysis
- Calculated confidence intervals for male and female customers.
- Compared the intervals.
- Evaluated differences in customer spending behavior.

### 6. Customer Segmentation
- Analyzed spending patterns by age group.
- Compared married and unmarried customers.
- Identified customer groups with higher spending.

## 📈 Key Findings

- **Purchase Distribution:** Customer purchase amounts show a right-skewed distribution.
- **Gender:** Male customers have a slightly higher average purchase than female customers, but the difference is small.
- **CLT:** The distribution of sample means appears approximately normal despite the skewed original distribution.
- **Confidence Intervals:** The male and female confidence intervals overlap, suggesting no clear statistically significant difference based on the report's analysis.
- **Marital Status:** Married customers tend to spend slightly more.
- **Age Group:** Customers aged 26–35 show higher spending compared with other age groups.
